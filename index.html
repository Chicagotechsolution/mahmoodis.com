Why a house, not a holding company

A holding company collects businesses. A house raises them. The difference is that a house says no more often: no to ventures that would spend the name faster than they earn it, no to announcements before the work exists, no to borrowing trust from one door to prop up another.

So this page will stay sparse for a while. Each entry in the register above becomes a link only when there is something real behind it. We'd rather you find five quiet lines here than fifty promises.

— The Mahmoodi family
